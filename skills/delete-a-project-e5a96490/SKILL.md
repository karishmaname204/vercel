# Delete a Project

## Inputs
- `{{project_name}}`: Project Name.

## Steps
1. Open https://vercel.com
2. focus
3. Enter {{project_name}}
4. Click text="{{project_name}}"
5. Click text="Settings"
6. scroll
7. Click text="Delete Project"
8. focus
9. Enter {{project_name}}
10. focus
11. Enter delete my project
12. Click text="Delete Project"
13. Check url
